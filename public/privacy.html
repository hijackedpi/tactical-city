Privacy Policy

Tactical City · tactical-city.com · Last updated 13 October 2026

Tactical City is a free browser game. This page explains what information the game keeps about you and why. You can play as a guest without giving us any information at all.

What we collect

  • Tactical City account: the username you choose and your password. Passwords are never stored as written; we only keep a one-way scrambled version (a salted hash) that cannot be turned back into your password.
  • Sign in with Google: your Google account ID, your name, your profile picture and your email address, as shared by Google when you choose to sign in.
  • Game data: your in-game name, your weapon loadout and the skins you own.
  • While playing: your in-game position, actions and chat messages are sent to the game server so the match can run. Chat messages are passed on to other players and are not stored.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to sign you in, show your name and picture, and keep your loadout and skins. We do not sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone else.

Cookies

When you sign in, the game sets one cookie that keeps you signed in on that browser for up to 180 days. The game also saves your settings (like graphics and sensitivity) in your own browser. Signing out removes the sign-in cookie.

Google sign-in

If you use Google to sign in, Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account. Tactical City's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy. You can remove the game's access at any time from your Google account's security settings.

Deleting your data

To have your account and everything attached to it deleted, email hijackedpi@gmail.com with your username.
